Since December 2025 **Immonation GmbH** has supported the athletics division of **TSV Zirndorf**. Managing Director **Erman Karabacak** stepped in personally as athlete sponsor for the two national squad athletes **Eni Kuske** and **Amelie Giese**, and additionally funds the division's youth work. This was not announced by our marketing department but by the club: the athletics division published the partnership on its own website on 14 December 2025.

[→ Read the original announcement by TSV Zirndorf](https://www.la-zirndorf.de/index.php/848-immonation-gmbh-wird-neuer-starker-partner)

## What the source states

Five points follow unambiguously from the announcement:

- **Timing:** published on 14 December 2025.
- **Partner:** the athletics division of TSV Zirndorf.
- **Athlete sponsorship:** Erman Karabacak personally, for Eni Kuske and Amelie Giese, both national squad athletes.
- **Purpose:** conditions for training, travel to competitions, and equipment.
- **In addition:** a further financial contribution for the division's entire youth programme.

In the announcement, Karabacak grounds the commitment in his own background:

> I competed seriously myself, so I know exactly how much financial support matters to young, committed athletes on their way. That is why it was a personal concern of mine to back not only the two top talents Amelie Giese and Eni Kuske, but the entire youth programme of TSV Zirndorf.

The German original reads: „Ich habe selbst früher leistungsorientiert Sport betrieben und weiß genau, wie wichtig die finanzielle Unterstützung für junge, engagierte Sportlerinnen auf ihrem Weg ist."

That the company is headquartered in Zirndorf is not a decision made from a rent index: Karabacak grew up here, still lives here, and deliberately chose his home town as the company's seat. That is what the source says – and it is the reason this sponsorship lands in Zirndorf rather than in a larger city with more reach.

## Two athletes, two disciplines

Eni Kuske, born 2007, runs the 400 metres. Amelie Giese, born 2010, competes in the long jump and over the hurdles. The source lists both as national squad athletes.

We do not rewrite what the two have achieved so far into prose. There are results cards for both athletes; they carry the status **July 2026** and appear here unchanged. Anyone who wants to check the placings reads them on the card – not in a summary we could have filtered.

## Not only the top

Athlete sponsorship is the part that tells well. The second part is less spectacular and arguably matters more: according to the announcement, the division receives a further financial contribution to support the entire youth programme and strengthen its foundational work.

That is the level a club actually lives on – training operations, coaching, kit for children who may never become national squad athletes. How much goes there is not in the source. That it goes there is.

## What is explicitly confidential

This is where the article parts company with a press release: the source itself says where it stops.

> Über die formalen Details und die Höhe der Unterstützung wurde zwischen den Parteien Stillschweigen vereinbart.

In English: the parties agreed to keep the formal details and the size of the support confidential. That is not a gap somebody forgot to fill – it is an **agreement**. So you will find no amount, no term, and no contract details here. And that is why this line stands here instead of us quietly passing over the question.

The same rule applies everywhere on this site: what we cannot evidence, or are not permitted to name, gets named as such rather than substituted – in [References](/en/references), in [Awards](/en/awards), and in the [education donation in Uganda](/en/news/uganda-education-donation).

## What this has to do with real estate

At first glance: nothing. At second glance: the same test.

Anyone selling a property hands one of the largest assets of their life to someone else – and has to decide beforehand whether the company keeps what it promises. There are exactly two kinds of evidence for that: claims and proof. A website can print any claim. A club that publishes a partnership with names, a date, and a quotation on its own site is proof.

That is precisely why this article takes this form: with a linked third-party source, with dated cards, with a video we do not narrate – and with an explicitly named point at which the public record ends.

## FAQ

### Who does Immonation GmbH support at TSV Zirndorf?

The athletics division of TSV Zirndorf. Managing Director Erman Karabacak personally sponsors the two national squad athletes Eni Kuske and Amelie Giese; a further contribution goes to the division's youth programme as a whole.

### Who confirms the partnership?

The athletics division of TSV Zirndorf. It announced the partnership on its own website on 14 December 2025, naming Immonation, Erman Karabacak, and both athletes. The post is freely readable.

### How large is the support?

That is not public. The source states explicitly that the parties agreed to keep the formal details and the size of the support confidential. We therefore name no amount and no term.

### Where do the athletes' results come from?

From the results cards for both athletes. They are embedded in this article as images and carry the status July 2026. We do not retype the placings into prose – the card is the evidence.

### Why does a real estate company fund athletics?

Because both are judged on the same thing: reliability over a long period. A sponsorship the club itself announces publicly and signs by name can be verified – a statement of values on your own website cannot.
