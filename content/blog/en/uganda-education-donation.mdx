In April 2026, **Immonation GmbH** supported the education work of the organisation **Pateka** in Uganda. The donation helps **cover school fees for students**. This is confirmed not by us but by the receiving organisation itself: Pateka documented the support publicly on 28 April 2026, naming both Immonation GmbH and Erman Karabacak.

[→ Read the original post by Pateka](https://pateka.de/de/2026/04/28/we-are-grateful-to-share-an-important-moment-for-our-community/)

## What is documented

Pateka describes in its own words what the support is used for and why it was needed at this point (the source is German; quotations here are our translation):

> This help comes at a time when many families face significant challenges in keeping their children in school.

Four points follow unambiguously from the source:

- **Timing:** April 2026, published on 28 April 2026.
- **Recipient:** Pateka, an organisation running education projects in Uganda.
- **Purpose:** a contribution towards school fees for students.
- **Named:** Immonation GmbH as the donor, and Erman Karabacak for his contribution.

In the same post, Pateka states that it deploys the funds responsibly and transparently, with the declared goal of giving every child a fair chance at education.

## Why school fees weigh heavily

Pateka describes the situation as one in which many families face significant challenges in keeping their children in school. Where school fees are part of that barrier, a contribution acts directly: it keeps open a place that would otherwise be lost. How widespread this is in detail is not quantified in the source – and we do not quantify it either.

Pateka puts the effect this way (translated from the German original):

> When a child can attend school, they gain not only knowledge but also confidence and new perspectives.

## What we explicitly do not claim

It would be easy to state a sum, a number of students, or a school here. We do not, because none of it appears in the source:

- **No donation amount.** The figure has not been published.
- **No student count.** How many children were reached does not follow from the post.
- **No school name and no duration.** Neither is named.
- **No impact figures.** What we cannot evidence, we do not publish – here as little as with our [references](/en/references) or [awards](/en/awards).

This is not modesty. It is the same standard we apply to every figure on this website.

## What this has to do with a real estate company

Anyone selling a property entrusts a company with one of the largest assets of their life. Nobody makes that decision on the basis of sympathy, but on the basis of verifiability: do the numbers hold? Can what is claimed be evidenced? Does someone put their name to it?

Community engagement follows the same rules. That is why there is no paragraph about values here, but a dated event with a named partner and a linked, independent source. You do not have to take our word for it – you can read it yourself.

## FAQ

### What did Immonation GmbH support in Uganda?

In April 2026 Immonation GmbH made a donation to the organisation Pateka that helps cover school fees for students in Uganda. Pateka documented the donation publicly on 28 April 2026.

### Who confirms the donation?

The receiving organisation itself. Pateka published the account on its own website and named both Immonation GmbH and Erman Karabacak. The post is freely readable.

### How large was the donation?

The amount has not been published. We state no sum, no number of students, and no school name, because none of it appears in the source and we do not publish unevidenced figures.

### Why does a real estate company support education?

Because the same standard applies as in daily business: commitments have to be verifiable. Support that a third party independently confirms and documents publicly can be checked – a statement of intent on our own website cannot.
