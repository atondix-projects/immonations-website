## How to spot fake reviews

Fake reviews are a growing problem — including in real estate. Use these signs to recognise fakes before you trust them:

- **Check the profile.** Only one review, or an empty profile with no history? Real reviewers usually have several contributions over time.
- **Look for concrete detail.** Genuine reviews name people, the process, or specific experiences. Fakes stay vague, overly positive, and without a clear connection.
- **Watch timing patterns.** Many five-star reviews within a few days often point to bought waves — real feedback spreads across months and years.
- **Question the language.** Heavy repetition of the company name, advertising slogans, or awkward, translation-like wording are typical warning signs.
- **Check the ratio.** Do the number and age of reviews match the company’s size and history? Hundreds of reviews overnight are suspicious.
- **Compare several sources.** Cross-check Google with other platforms. A consistent picture across portals supports authenticity.
